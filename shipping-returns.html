Shipping & Returns

Delivery and return policy for Mumbaa Ceramic Studio

Shipping

We ship across India. Orders are processed within 2–3 business days. Delivery typically takes 5–7 business days from dispatch, depending on your location.

Free shipping on orders above ₹1,000. For orders below that, a flat shipping charge of ₹100 applies.

You will receive an email with tracking details once your order is dispatched. Ceramics are packed with care to prevent damage in transit.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in original packaging. To start a return, contact us at info@mumbaaceramicstudio.com with your order number.

Once we receive the returned item and confirm its condition, we will process a refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, or arrange an exchange.

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was defective or incorrect.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost.
