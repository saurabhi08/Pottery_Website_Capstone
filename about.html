Our Mission

We believe in the beauty of slow creation and the value of handmade objects. Our mission is to create functional art that enriches daily life while supporting local artisans and sustainable practices.

Each piece tells a story of dedication, skill, and the timeless craft of pottery. We work with locally sourced materials and eco-friendly glazes, ensuring that our beautiful ceramics are as kind to the earth as they are to your home.

The Creation Process

  1. Shaping: Each piece begins on the pottery wheel, where clay is centred and pulled into simple, functional forms designed for daily use.
  2. Drying & Trimming: Once leather-hard, we refine the shape, add details and trim the foot for a comfortable, stable finish.
  3. First Firing (Bisque): Pieces are fired in the kiln to harden the clay and prepare the surface for glazing.
  4. Glazing: We apply small-batch glazes by hand, layering colours and finishes to achieve the look and feel we want.
  5. Final Firing: A second firing at high temperature creates a smooth, glass-like surface that is durable and food-safe.
